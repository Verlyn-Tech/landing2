## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of growing small and medium businesses in the UAE who are too small to hire an in-house tech team or afford a big agency. They want automation but lack the budget and skills to set it up, and they are numb to agency buzzwords.

The primary audience is broad UAE SMEs, not one vertical. Specific industries (the live site's independent cafes, specialty roasteries and automotive workshops; the strategy's clinics, small real-estate brokers, F&B, logistics and trading firms) are examples of that audience, not the definition of it.

Their situation: leads arrive on WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, website forms and ads, live in staff phones, and get answered slowly or not at all while the owner and staff are busy doing the actual work of the business.

The job they hire Verlyn for: stop losing leads and hours to manual work, without becoming the "systems person" themselves.

## Product Purpose

Verlyn Tech is a growth-systems team that installs the operational systems growing UAE businesses are missing (fast lead response, automated follow-up, clean data, fewer manual hours) and keeps them running.

Nobody buys "AI". Clients buy faster lead response, fewer dropped customers, less admin time and cleaner data for decisions. Automation and AI are the mechanism, never the pitch.

Success for a client is a before-and-after number from their own business (response time, leads with no owner, hours saved, revenue recovered). Success for Verlyn is turning proven pilots into monthly retainers.

## Positioning

Verlyn installs the system, proves it with the client's own numbers in a small pilot, and keeps it running after launch. It sits between five competitor types, each with a nameable weakness:

- Enterprise consultancies: same category of system in weeks, not quarters, at a fraction of the price.
- Generic "AI agency" shops: a before-and-after number from the client's own pilot instead of unverifiable homepage claims.
- DIY no-code platforms (ManyChat, Wati, Zoko, Voiceflow): built, tested and maintained for the client, so they never have to learn it.
- Single-function specialists: one connected system (ad, landing page, WhatsApp reply and CRM owned by one team) instead of three vendors to blame.
- Solo freelancers: documented SOPs, a security review and a team behind it, not one person's inbox.

## Operating Context

- Market: UAE SMEs. Copy and systems in English, with Arabic available (native-reviewed) where the client needs it.
- Channels clients already live in: WhatsApp Business, Instagram, website forms, Meta/Google/TikTok ads, Google Business Profile reviews.
- Tools Verlyn installs: HubSpot free tier, Zoho or Airtable CRMs; Make, Zapier or n8n automation; WhatsApp Business API; Framer, Webflow or custom HTML/CSS pages; GA4 and Search Console; LLM APIs for bots and agents. Clients own their accounts, credentials and data.
- Sales motion: founder-led outbound (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, DMs) opening with a specific gap noticed in the prospect's business; free zones, coworking hubs and SME programmes (Dubai SME, Sheraa, ADSME) as trust channels.
- Engagement shape: discovery and mapping of the customer flow, a small pilot, a measured before-and-after, then a monthly retainer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services on the live site (index.html): CRM setup and lead management; landing pages and SEO; marketing and paid ads; lead generation; ERP setup; premium web development. Deliverables described there: static sub-second web presence with local SEO markup, official WhatsApp Business API gateway with automated routing, and post-service Google review requests.

The strategy document (assets/Verlyn_Tech_Strategy_v2 (1).pdf) defines a different six: CRM, landing pages and SEO, paid ads, lead generation, agentic AI, and AI automation, plus possible future products (Revenue Leak Radar first, then vertical products such as ClinicFlow). Future products are ideas, not shipped software.

Current public offer: a 14-day pilot, maximum three businesses onboarded per cycle, with a guarantee of a full refund if lead response speed does not double. The guarantee wording is confirmed.

Constraints:
- Every messaging flow includes consent-based opt-in and opt-out.
- Keys, logins and stored chat data are locked down on every install; money and commitments require human approval in any agent.
- The contact form on the site does not submit anywhere yet.

Open decisions (do not resolve in copy without the owner):
- Which six services are canonical: the site's set (with ERP and premium web development) or the strategy's set (with agentic AI and AI automation).
- Pilot length: the site says 14 days; the strategy proposes a 30-day paid pilot credited against the first retainer month.
- Whether pricing is ever shown publicly. The strategy's AED ranges and packages are untested starting hypotheses, not published prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Verlyn Tech. Bespoke VT wing logo and wordmark in assets/logos/ (horizontal, stacked, emblem; dark and light variants).
- Never pitched as an "AI agency".
- Voice: specific and numbers-first, plain English, calm confidence. Banned language: AI-powered, cutting-edge, game-changing, leverage synergies, revolutionize, disrupt, next-gen, unlock the power of AI, seamless AI integration. If a sentence could appear on a thousand other agency sites, cut it.
- Reports and claims lead with the number that matters to the owner; only concrete, provable claims.
- The live site's visual world and the rules in README.md are the incumbent visual authority; that belongs to DESIGN.md work, not here.

## Evidence on Hand

- The site's "0 unanswered inquiries" and "14.5h billable hours recovered each week" are illustrative scenarios, not measurements. Future work must label them as examples or remove them, and must not present any figure as a client result until one exists.
- Market statistics in the strategy (94% of UAE businesses are SMEs, 8% at advanced digital maturity, enterprise AI spend up 105% year over year) come from that document; cite a source before using them publicly.
- Real assets: logo set (assets/logos/), hero photography (assets/hero/v3/basalt_crest.jpg), generated cafe and garage scene photography (assets/img/, currently not used on the page), strategy document (assets/).

## Product Principles

1. Sell the outcome, never the technology. Lead with response time, hours and money, not with AI.
2. Prove before scaling. Every engagement starts small and earns the next phase with the client's own before-and-after number.
3. One connected system, one accountable team, from ad to CRM.
4. Build it so the client never has to become the systems person, and keep it running after launch.
5. Truth over polish: no invented numbers, clients or claims, even when the page would look better with them.
